# Macro-nutrient Distribution Rules

Once the daily calorie target is determined, it is divided into three main macro-nutrients: Protein, Fats, and Carbohydrates.

## General Guidelines
Standard caloric values for macros:
- **Protein:** 4 calories per gram.
- **Carbohydrates:** 4 calories per gram.
- **Fats:** 9 calories per gram.

## Distribution Ratios
Based on goals, use the following distributions:

### 1. Balanced (Maintenance)
- **Protein:** 25-30%
- **Fats:** 25-30%
- **Carbs:** 40-50%

### 2. High Protein (Muscle Building / Fat Loss)
- **Protein:** 35-40%
- **Fats:** 20-25%
- **Carbs:** 35-45%
- *Rule of thumb:* 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kg of body weight.

### 3. Low Carb / Keto
- **Protein:** 20-25%
- **Fats:** 65-75%
- **Carbs:** 5-10% (typically < 50g per day)

## Calculation Steps
1. Determine total daily calories.
2. Choose a ratio (e.g., 40% P, 30% F, 30% C).
3. Calculate calories for each macro: `Total Calories * Ratio`.
4. Convert to grams: `Calories / (4 or 9)`.
